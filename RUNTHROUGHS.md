# BorrowIQ — Three Borrower Run-Throughs

This document demonstrates how BorrowIQ assesses the three challenge personas: Priya, Ravi, and Anita. 

The application uses adaptive questioning powered by the AI Question Selector. It asks only the questions necessary to determine safe cash-flow capacity and fair pricing bands.

---

## 1. Priya (Age 29, Bengaluru)

**Profile:** Software engineer. Net income: ₹1,10,000/month. Existing car EMI: ₹14,000. Rent: ₹28,000. Credit score: 780. She wants an ₹8,00,000 personal loan for a wedding.

**Questions the Application Asked:**
1. **Loan Purpose:** Wedding / Personal Event
2. **Amount Wanted:** ₹8,00,000
3. **Age:** 29
4. **Employment Type:** Salaried (Corporate / MNC)
5. **Net Monthly Income:** ₹1,10,000
6. **Existing Monthly EMIs:** ₹14,000
7. **Household Living Expenses:** ₹28,000
8. **Credit Score:** 750 and above
9. **Adaptive Question (AI Selected):** *What percentage of your annual compensation is variable or bonus?*
   * *AI Selection Rationale:* Evaluates potential volatility in corporate bonus pay to ensure the safe EMI ceiling holds during lean quarters.
   * *Answer:* 0%–15%

*(The AI Question Selector stops here. It does not ask Priya about business vintage, unencumbered commercial property, or app loan defaults because she is salaried with a 780 score. Those variables do not alter her prime pricing band).*

**The Four Outputs:**
1. **Verdict:** Borrow.
   * *Reason:* Stable corporate cash flow, low leverage (12.7% FOIR), and clean credit bureau record comfortably support this loan.
2. **Maximum Amount:**
   * *Lender Estimate:* ₹15,00,000 to ₹18,00,000 (Banks use 60% FOIR over 60 months).
   * *Borrower Safe Limit:* ₹9,00,000 to ₹11,00,000 (Using safe 35% FOIR over 36–48 months).
   * *Advice:* Use the Safe Limit. Her requested ₹8,00,000 fits comfortably inside it.
3. **Fair Interest Rate:** 
   * *Fair Band:* 10.25% to 12.0%. 
   * *All-In APR:* ~11.5% including a 1% processing fee and 18% GST.
4. **EMI Ceiling:**
   * *Maximum Safe EMI:* ₹24,500/month.
   * *Stress Test:* If her income drops by 20%, her debt ratio rises to 44%, which is stretched but survivable.

## 2. Ravi (Age 42, Mysuru)

**Profile:** Kirana store owner for 14 years. Cash income: ₹40,000 to ₹80,000/month. Tax return (ITR) shows ₹4,20,000/year. Owns unencumbered shop premises worth ₹45,00,000. No formal credit score. Wife earns ₹18,000 teaching. He wants ₹15,00,000 for store expansion and a delivery vehicle.

**Questions the Application Asked:**
1. **Loan Purpose:** Business Growth / Stock
2. **Amount Wanted:** ₹15,00,000
3. **Age:** 42
4. **Employment Type:** Business Owner / Kirana
5. **Net Monthly Income:** ₹68,000 (Combined average household income)
6. **Existing Monthly EMIs:** ₹0
7. **Household Living Expenses:** ₹25,000
8. **Credit Score:** I do not know my score
9. **Adaptive Question 1 (AI Selected):** *Do you or your family own clear-title, unencumbered property?*
   * *AI Selection Rationale:* Evaluates whether unencumbered real estate can unlock a 9.0%–10.5% Loan Against Property (LAP) instead of an expensive 16%+ personal loan.
   * *Answer:* Yes, commercial shop premises.
10. **Adaptive Question 2 (AI Selected):** *What is the approximate market value of this property?*
    * *AI Selection Rationale:* Measures property valuation to calculate exact 50%–60% LTV sanction capacity.
    * *Answer:* ₹45,00,000.
11. **Adaptive Question 3 (AI Selected):** *How many continuous years has your business or shop been active?*
    * *AI Selection Rationale:* 10+ years operating history demonstrates cash-flow resilience and offsets missing bureau credit files (-50 bps credit).
    * *Answer:* 14 years.

**The Four Outputs:**
1. **Verdict:** Borrow (Secured Route).
   * *Reason:* Pledging unencumbered shop property worth ₹45L unlocks an MSME Loan Against Property (LAP) at 9.0%–10.5% over 7–10 years, cutting monthly payments in half compared to unsecured credit.
2. **Maximum Amount:**
   * *Lender Estimate (Unsecured):* ₹8,00,000 (Capped by ₹4.2L ITR).
   * *Lender Estimate (Secured LAP):* ₹22,50,000 (50% LTV of ₹45L premises).
   * *Borrower Safe Limit:* ₹12,00,000 to ₹15,00,000.
3. **Fair Interest Rate:** 
   * *Fair Band:* 9.25% to 11.5% (Secured LAP pricing schedule with vintage credit).
4. **EMI Ceiling:**
   * *Maximum Safe EMI:* ₹23,800/month.
   * *Stress Test:* Fluctuation in kirana sales is cushioned by using a conservative debt limit.

## 3. Anita (Age 35, Hubballi)

**Profile:** Delivery rider and home tailoring. Net income: ₹26,000 to ₹30,000/month. Husband unemployed for 8 months. Three instant app loans totaling ₹35,000 outstanding at 30%+ interest, with 1 recent bounce. Wants ₹1,50,000 for an electric scooter.

**Questions the Application Asked:**
1. **Loan Purpose:** Vehicle or Machinery Purchase
2. **Amount Wanted:** ₹1,50,000
3. **Age:** 35
4. **Employment Type:** Gig Platform / Informal / Freelancer
5. **Net Monthly Income:** ₹28,000 (Average)
6. **Existing Monthly EMIs:** ₹8,000
7. **Household Living Expenses:** ₹20,000
8. **Credit Score:** Below 650
9. **Adaptive Question 1 (AI Selected):** *Are any of your current loans from instant apps or private lenders at 30%+?*
   * *AI Selection Rationale:* Identifies active high-cost debt that drains monthly surplus and triggers acute debt-spiral risk.
   * *Answer:* Yes (Servicing instant app loans at 30%+).
10. **Adaptive Question 2 (AI Selected):** *Have you had any missed due dates or bounced EMIs in the past 6 months?*
    * *AI Selection Rationale:* Verifies repayment stability and automated rejection risk at formal banks.
    * *Answer:* Yes, 1 bounce last month.

**The Four Outputs:**
1. **Verdict:** Do Not Borrow Yet.
   * *Reason:* You are servicing high-cost 30%+ instant app loans with a recent bounce and zero monthly cash surplus. Adding commercial debt now guarantees a debt spiral.
   * *Actionable Alternative:* Refinance and consolidate high-cost app loans through a Self-Help Group (SHG) or microfinance institution (MFI) at 12%–15% before acquiring vehicle debt.
2. **Maximum Amount:**
   * *Lender Estimate:* ₹0 (Tier-1 banks reject based on active bounce and debt ratio breach).
   * *Borrower Safe Limit:* ₹0 (Current cash-flow floor is zero/negative).
3. **Fair Interest Rate:** 
   * *Fair Band:* Commercial personal loans should not be taken. Formal MFI debt consolidation is fair at 12%–15%.
4. **EMI Ceiling:**
   * *Maximum Safe EMI:* ₹0/month.
   * *Reason:* Household expenses (₹20,000) and existing debt (₹8,000) consume 100% of income (₹28,000). Zero uncommitted margin exists.
